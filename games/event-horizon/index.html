Controls & field notes

Drag on the star field to aim, or use the angle slider. Left and right arrows adjust by one degree; hold Shift for a tenth of a degree. Space or Enter releases light when the field is focused. R resets the chamber. Every chamber is available from the selector.

Light curves towards each dark centre. Its direction changes in small, fixed steps, with a softened pull and a capped turn. Touching a dark core or a pale barrier ends the path. This is a visual illustration, not a simulation of real physics. Previous paths remain faintly visible to help you adjust.
