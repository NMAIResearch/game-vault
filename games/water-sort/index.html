About this game

Water Sort for Game Vault. Progress and preferences are saved only in this browser. No account, tracking or external requests.

TAKE IT ONE POUR AT A TIME

Make room for colour.

  1. Pick a tube. Tap or click the colour you want to move.
  2. Choose where it goes. Pour onto the same top colour, or into an empty tube. Only the top block moves, as far as the space allows.
  3. Find a little order. Finish with each colour in its own full tube. Empty tubes can stay empty.

Undo and hints are always free. Each puzzle also has one free spare tube if you need more room.

Use Tab and Enter or Space to select tubes. Arrow keys move between them. Escape clears a selection. With focus inside the puzzle panel, U undoes, H gives a hint and R opens restart.

Turn on Symbols to tell colours apart by shape. Your progress is saved on this browser when storage is available.

Day and Night change the colours; the game starts with your device’s setting. On phones that support it, Vibration gives a short buzz when you pour, when a pour is refused and when you finish a level.
