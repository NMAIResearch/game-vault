How claims are checked
  • Points: pawn 1, knight 3, bishop 3, rook 5, queen 9.
  • Hanging: if it were my move, I could take that piece and come out ahead in points after both sides finish recapturing on its square.
  • Attacks: my piece could move to that square if it were my turn, ignoring pins.
  • Mate in one: if you passed, I would have a move that is checkmate.
  • Castling: whether you still have the right to castle, even if the way is blocked right now.
